# Paperworks support handbook

This is an original fictional company created only for the Chatify demonstration. It is not a real vendor policy.

## Standard delivery
Orders leave the workshop within two business days after design approval. Tracking is emailed when the parcel is handed to the courier. A tracking number alone does not prove delivery.

## Personalised prints
Customers approve a digital proof before a personalised print is produced. Changes before approval are included. After approval, support reviews requests individually and does not promise that production can stop.

## Damaged parcels
Ask for an order number and clear photos of the parcel and print within seven calendar days of delivery. Support reviews the evidence before deciding a replacement. The handbook does not promise refunds for every case.

## Returns
Unused standard prints may be returned within fourteen calendar days of delivery. Personalised prints are not covered by the standard return window unless damaged or incorrectly produced.
